## The image

A hyper-excited golden retriever leaps straight toward the viewer, huge and centered, its face filling the upper-center of the frame — mouth wide open in a huge joyful smile, ears flying back, eyes sparkling, all four legs tucked into the leap. Bursts of colorful confetti and golden fireworks explode behind it as a celebration backdrop, lit dramatically and cinematically. It targets the judge's criteria in order: the dog flying at the camera is the instant jolt of excitement; a joyful dog bursting out of a firework celebration is the surprise twist; the tucked leap and flying ears carry the momentum; the render stays clean with both eyes visible and no text anywhere; and at thumbnail size the big smiling face and paws still hit (craft, reads small). The dog takes up most of the frame, so the fireworks stay celebration behind it rather than the subject — the leap itself is the surprise, not the explosions.
